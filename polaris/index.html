How to read Polaris

Strong claims earn their way onto the page.

01

Public events

Game events, shifts, and roster context establish the observable layer.

02

In-house models

Chance quality, role adjustment, and defensive context turn events into comparable reads.

03

Semantic checks

Every displayed metric declares whether higher, lower, or neutral context is the correct reading.

04

Publication gates

Thin samples, unstable defensive signals, and unsupported language are caveated or suppressed.
