Retired

Up 88.7% before fees. Then the market changed.

An automated strategy across Kalshi event markets. When its best market went quiet and its sizing drifted, Cam switched it off instead of trading through it. The strategy is retired.

Won 74.6% of trades. Currency contracts went 28-3 across 31 trades (90.3%) and made 130% of the net profit. The other 438 trades lost money together.

After the pause June 26 to September 17, 2026

A smaller index book kept running after the original strategy was switched off: 62 trades (47 S&P 500, 14 Nasdaq, 1 currency), won-lost 38-24 (61.3%), for -9.0% on deposited capital and -10.2% per amount staked, both before fees. It is reported apart because it is not the strategy above, and it is not where the edge was.

Return on deposited capital
Net profit divided by the total deposited into the account. It is not divided by the amount staked, which was many times larger, so the return per amount staked is shown beside it.
Trades won-lost
A trade is won when it closed at a profit, whether it was held to settlement or sold before it.
Currency contracts
Kalshi markets on where an exchange rate, such as EUR/USD or USD/JPY, sits at a set time.
Return per amount staked
Net profit divided by the total amount put into trades. The deposited capital was staked many times over, which is why this is far smaller than the return on deposited capital.
Currency share of profit
Currency trades' profit as a share of the original strategy's whole net profit. Above 100% means every other trade, taken together, lost money.
Fees
Not included yet. Every figure on this page is before Kalshi trading fees, which the trade ledger does not record. A net figure, from the account statements, will replace the headline once it is computed.

How it went

  1. Early run

    Currency contracts carry the book

    Currency trades made more than all of the profit, traded with tight position sizing.

  2. Late April

    Currency orders stop filling

    The best market drops out, and the rest of the strategy doesn't adjust.

  3. May to June

    Momentum regime, size drift

    Sustained momentum exposes the remaining trades, and positions grow past their limits.

  4. June 22

    Switched off

    Stopped rather than trade through it.

What carries forward

Hard position caps

Sizing limits enforced in code, so they can't drift.

Fill-failure alert

An alert the moment orders stop filling, so a market can't go quiet unnoticed.
