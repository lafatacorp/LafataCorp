About

Dallas born, Boston raised, and back home talking hockey on KTCK 96.7/1310 The Ticket.

On air

Cam covers hockey and Dallas sports on The Ticket, and works on camera and in the studio with SMUTV. Long-form conversation goes out through Pony Up Productions, the SMU student podcast. Clips from all three are being collected on the Media page.

School and research

Cam studies Sport Management at SMU, across the Simmons School and the Cox School of Business. His research looks at legalized sports betting and prediction markets as investments: what the prices say, where they are wrong, and whether that can be measured rather than guessed at.

LafataCorp

LafataCorp is where that research turns into working systems. The NHL Tracker posts paper-traded positions every morning with the reasoning behind each one. Polaris is a hockey analytics platform built in the open, Dallas first. Fantasy is the weekly work behind more than fifteen years of fantasy hockey, published as it happens.

What is next

The plan is hockey operations.
