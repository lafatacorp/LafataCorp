Is it really this easy to bet every favorite?

Favorites Tracker, 2026-27 regular season

The tracker buys the pre-game favorite in every game priced at 56% or more and asks whether that alone makes money. Stakes are Kelly sized, one and a half times the Kelly fraction for an edge assumed to grow with the price, off the current balance and up to a fixed cap per bet. The balance has been big enough that 166 of 217 bets this season hit the cap, so in practice it has mostly staked a flat amount. Every return here is measured against the bankroll it started the season with.
