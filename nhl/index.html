Method

How each system decides

01

Daily Analyzer

Builds a win probability for every game on the slate from expected goals, high-danger chance share, shooting luck, rest and travel, home and road splits, and head-to-head history. It compares that to the market’s implied probability and posts a side only when the gap clears its threshold. Games it declines are logged too.

02

Fade Bot

Watches live games for a team protecting a one-goal lead and scores the pressure against them from shot quality in the preceding minutes. It takes the leading side when the pressure reading and the time remaining both sit in a range with a documented hold rate, and sizes smaller as the entry price rises.

03

Daily reasoning

The paragraph under each play is generated from the exact signals the model recorded for that game — nothing else is fed in and nothing is added by hand. Each signal is labelled with the period it comes from, so a prior-season split or a preseason run is never passed off as current form.
