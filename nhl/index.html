Favorites Tracker, 2026-27 regular season

The tracker buys the pre-game favorite in every game priced at 56% or more and asks whether that alone makes money. Stakes are Kelly sized, one and a half times the Kelly fraction for an edge assumed to grow with the price, off the current balance and up to a fixed cap per bet. The balance has been big enough that 147 of 195 bets this season hit the cap, so in practice it has mostly staked a flat amount. Nov 13 to Nov 19

20-15 across 35 positions. The Favorites Tracker went 10-11 (-37.8%), Lead Hold 5-2 (+2.4%) and The LafataCorp Model 5-2 (+2.9%).

5 of those 35 got past regulation, going 2-3. A game that reaches overtime has already slipped out of the model's hands: the read was on sixty minutes and what follows is close to a coin flip.

Season to date Lead Hold is 9-17 once a game passes regulation and 39-9 when it does not. Those overtimes are near enough a coin flip, and the coin has not been landing this way by design.

Where this stands. Lead Hold finished 2025-26 at 93-28, but the tracker only came online for the back half of that season, so there is no full-season record behind it. This season is the first being gathered from opening night, which is the point of publishing it. Every system here is paper traded, the figures are unaudited, and the model is still being developed in the open. Expect the numbers to move.
