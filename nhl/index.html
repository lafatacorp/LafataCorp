Favorites Tracker, 2026-27 regular season

The tracker buys the pre-game favorite in every game priced at 56% or more and asks whether that alone makes money. Stakes are Kelly sized, one and a half times the Kelly fraction for an edge assumed to grow with the price, off the current balance and up to a fixed cap per bet. The balance has been big enough that 165 of 216 bets this season hit the cap, so in practice it has mostly staked a flat amount. Every return here is measured against the bankroll it started the season with.

This week All weeks

Nov 18 to Nov 24

22-19 across 41 positions. The Favorites Tracker went 13-9 (-1.9%), Lead Hold 4-4 (-5.5%) and The LafataCorp Model 5-6 (-6.5%).

9 of those 41 got past regulation, going 1-8. A game that reaches overtime has already slipped out of the model's hands: the read was on sixty minutes and what follows is close to a coin flip.

Season to date Lead Hold is 10-18 once a game passes regulation and 41-12 when it does not. Those overtimes are near enough a coin flip, and the coin has not been landing this way by design.

Where this stands. Lead Hold finished 2025-26 at 93-28, but the tracker only came online for the back half of that season, so there is no full-season record behind it. This season is the first being gathered from opening night, which is the point of publishing it. Every system here is paper traded, the figures are unaudited, and the model is still being developed in the open. Expect the numbers to move.
