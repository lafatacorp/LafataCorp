Hit counts are games over 2.5 shots on goal, the line these props are most often hung at. It is a fixed reference, not a quote from a book, and nothing here is a price. The projection blends a skater's season, last-ten and last-five shot rates, then multiplies by how many shots tonight's opponent allows against a league average of 27.8 per game. An opponent factor above 1.00 means they give up more shots than average. Ranked by projection.
